CRAFT & SCIENCE

Material & Care Science

Understanding natural Jaipur clay composition, thermal shock resistance, and non-toxic mineral glaze standards.

MINERAL PURITY

Organic Earthen Terracotta

Our clay is harvested from deep alluvial deposits in Rajasthan. Rich in calcium, magnesium, and iron oxides, natural unglazed clay interacts with steam during cooking to enhance natural dish aromas.

SAFETY CERTIFICATION

100% Non-Toxic Mineral Glazes

Many commercial ceramics rely on lead or cadmium fluxes to lower firing temperatures. Ceramic Kitchen glazes are formulated purely from ground quartz, feldspar, and non-reactive earth minerals vitrified at 1200°C.

Zero Lead Zero Cadmium Zero PTFE / PFOA NABL Certified

Deep Thermal Retention

Clay holds heat twice as long as stainless steel, keeping curries piping hot at the dining table for up to 45 minutes.

Self-Basting Steam Loop

Pores in the lid trap aromatics and drip moisture continuously back onto simmering vegetables and lentils.

Thermal Shock Guard

Vitrified at 1200°C for exceptional resistance to heat expansion on stovetops and inside ovens.

MINERAL SCIENCE

Vitrification & Quartz Density

At temperatures between 1180°C and 1220°C, natural quartz sand and clay silica particles melt and fuse together into a dense, stone-like matrix. This eliminates liquid absorption while preserving the clay's far-infrared thermal emission properties.

<0.5% Porosity Non-Absorbent Surface
Natural Quartz Zero Chemical Synthetic Flux

DAILY MAINTENANCE

Cleaning & Storage Journal

Maintaining earthen ceramics is simple. Wash with warm water and soft coconut fiber sponge. Avoid harsh chemical detergents or synthetic bleach. Allow pots to air dry completely before storing in open wooden kitchen racks.

✓ Warm Water Rinse ✓ Air Dry in Sunlight

First-Time Seasoning Steps

Step 1: Soak

Submerge the new pot in clean water for 8-12 hours before initial cooking.

Step 2: Oil Coat

Rub unrefined mustard or sesame oil into interior surfaces and air dry.

Step 3: Low Simmer

Simmer rice water on low heat for 15 mins to seal natural clay pores.
