CULINARY SCIENCE

The Magic of Claypot Slow Cooking

Cooking in unglazed earthenware is unlike any metal pot. Clay's natural alkaline composition neutralizes acidity in tomatoes and citrus, producing sweeter, creamier, and more aromatic curries and stews without excessive fat or artificial seasoning.

Natural Alkalinity Balances Dish Acidity
Self-Basting Steam Locks Herbal Aromatics
BAKING TECHNIQUES

Baking Oven-Spring Sourdough in Terracotta

Terracotta Dutch ovens trap steam released by dough during the first 20 minutes of baking. This prevents early crust hardening, allowing maximum oven spring and yielding an exceptionally crisp, blistered artisanal sourdough crust.
