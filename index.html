ARTISAN CRAFT

Handmolded Terracotta Built for Slow Cooking

Unlike metal pans that scorch and leach chemicals, natural clay breathes heat evenly. Our kitchenware uses organic lead-free mineral glazes for non-reactive, effortless cooking.

OUR CRAFT RITUAL

From Jaipur Kilns to Your Table

Every piece undergoes a 3-stage handcrafting process rooted in ancient Rajasthani pottery traditions.

STAGE 01

Alluvial Clay Wedging

Harvested deep from riverbeds, our natural terracotta clay is kneaded by hand to eliminate air pockets and ensure optimal heat retention.

STAGE 02

1200°C Kiln Firing

Slowly vitrified in traditional brick kilns for 18 hours to achieve maximum thermal shock resistance without chemical synthetic hardeners.

STAGE 03

Mineral Glaze Bath

Dipped in 100% lead-free quartz and natural oxide glazes for non-reactive, non-stick cooking that neutralizes food acidity naturally.
