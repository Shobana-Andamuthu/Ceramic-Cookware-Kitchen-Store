SLOW SIMMER RECIPE

Harvest Vegetable Stew in Claypot

A comforting blend of roasted sweet potatoes, lentils, and garden herbs slow-simmered inside an earthen terracotta pot.

Prep: 15 mins
Cook: 90 mins
Serves: 4 Persons
Difficulty: Easy

Fresh Ingredients

  • • Diced sweet potatoes & carrots 300g
  • • Brown lentils (soaked) 1 Cup
  • • Cold-pressed mustard oil 2 Tbsp
  • • Cumin seeds & crushed garlic 1 tsp
  • • Vegetable broth 3 Cups
  • • Fresh rosemary sprigs 2 sprigs

Preparation Method

Step 1: Tempering Spices

Heat mustard oil in your clay pot over low flame. Add cumin seeds and crushed garlic until golden aroma fills the room.

Step 2: Sautéing Vegetables

Toss in sweet potatoes and carrots. Stir gently for 5 minutes as clay absorbs and radiates heat.

Step 3: Slow Simmering

Add soaked lentils, vegetable broth, and rosemary. Cover with clay lid and simmer on low heat for 80 minutes.

Stovetop Safety

Heat Management for Terracotta Cookware

Unlike metal pans, clay responds to heat slowly. Follow these flame levels to keep your terracotta intact for years.

1. Low Heat (First 5 Mins)

Always start on a low flame. This allows the terracotta body to expand uniformly and prevents micro-fractures.

2. Medium Heat (Simmering)

Increase flame to medium once steam starts building. Terracotta holds heat exceptionally well, making high heat unnecessary.

3. Avoid High Flame

Direct intense flame on a single dry point can cause thermal shock. Never use high heat directly on empty pots.

4. Cool-Down Period

Never place a piping hot pot directly on cold marble or submerge it in cold water. Let it cool on a wooden trivet.
