CARE GUIDE

First-Time Seasoning Steps

Step 1: Water Soak

Submerge pot and lid in clean tap water for 12 hours before initial use.

Step 2: Mustard Oil Coat

Rub unglazed interior with organic edible oil and let dry thoroughly.

Step 3: Low Heat Warm-Up

Simmer rice water on low flame for 30 minutes to seal clay micro-pores.

Step 4: Gentle Care

Hand wash with hot water and soft brush. Avoid harsh chemical soap powders.

Handmade Lifecycle

The 8-Day Pot Clay Cycle

Every single terracotta pot and lid undergoes a meticulous week-long journey before leaving our studio workshop.

1

Clay Sifting & Aging

Jaipur red clay is sun-dried, crushed, sifted, and aged for 48 hours with river quartz sand for thermal stability.

2

Kick-Wheel Shaping

Potters center and shape each vessel body on manual kick-wheels, trimming walls to exact thermal-density specs.

3

Slow Sun Drying

Before kiln firing, pots dry slowly in our open courtyard for 3 days to release natural moisture without cracking.

4

Kiln Firing & Glaze

Pots are coated in natural lead-free mineral dust and vitrified in our wood-fired kiln at 1180°C for 18 hours.
