FIRE & ATMOSPHERE

Wood Kiln Firing & Vitrification

Witnessing a firing night in Sanganer is pure magic. As temperatures cross 1200°C, wood embers dance around terracotta walls, permanently locking natural mineral colors into the ceramic surface.

RAW MATERIALS

Natural Earth Pigments & Mineral Glazes

Our glaze lab uses only ground quartz, white clay, cobalt oxides, and crushed stone to achieve subtle earth tones. Zero lead, zero synthetic plastic resins.

HOME LIFESTYLE

Handcrafted Clay in Modern Homes

From open wooden pantry shelves to rustic dining tables, warm earthenware adds organic texture, serene grounding, and timeless beauty to modern living spaces.

BEHIND THE WHEEL

Preserving Earthen Heritage

Each ceramic piece tells a story of patient craftsmanship. From wedging raw Jaipur clay to hand-applying lead-free glazes and firing at 1200°C.
