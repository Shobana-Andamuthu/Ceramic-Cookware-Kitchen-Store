Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers regarding terracotta care, gas stove safety, and courier deliveries.

Submerge the clay pot completely in clean room-temperature water for 8-12 hours. Wipe dry, rub the interior with organic rice water or sesame oil, and heat gently over low flame for 15 minutes before cooking.
Our pots are 100% safe for direct gas flames (use low-to-medium heat) and ovens up to 260°C. For induction cooktops, use a flat stainless steel induction diffuser plate.
