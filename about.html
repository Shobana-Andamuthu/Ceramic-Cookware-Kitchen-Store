OUR HERITAGE

Crafting Earthen Heritage

Founded in Jaipur with a vision to replace synthetic Teflon cookware with pure, non-toxic terracotta clay pieces.

HANDMADE ETHOS

Slow Pottery for Slow Meals

In a fast-paced world, we believe in taking time. Every cooking pot is wedged, shaped on kick-wheels, air-dried under Jaipur sunshine, and fired in wood kilns for over 18 hours.

OUR STANDARDS

Our Studio Commitments

100% Non-Toxic

Certified free from lead, cadmium, PFOA, and heavy metal fluxes by NABL laboratory testing.

Fair Wage Studio

Empowering traditional artisan families with sustainable wages, health coverage, and workshop pensions.

Zero-Plastic Shipping

Packed carefully using recycled honeycomb paper wrap, biodegradable straw, and cotton twine tie.

FIRE & EARTH SCIENCE

18-Hour Kiln Firing Mastery

Our traditional brick kilns are stoked by hand using renewable wood trimmings. Reaching temperatures exceeding 1200°C, the intense heat fuses raw quartz minerals directly into the earthen body—creating cookware that endures generations of gas stove cooking.

1200°C Vitrification Temp
100% Organic Rajasthani Alluvial Clay
SUSTAINABILITY

Lifetime Re-Glazing & Circular Pottery

Unlike disposable Teflon pans that end up in landfills after a few years, earthen ceramics can be re-seasoned, restored, and used for decades. We offer studio re-glazing services and lifetime care support for every pot sold.

  • Biodegradable natural clay composition
  • Free lifetime pottery seasoning advice
  • Zero synthetic chemical binders
