Our Safe Transit Packaging Guarantee

Ceramics are fragile, but our custom engineered honeycomb paper system ensures your items arrive completely intact.

1. Double-Wall Kraft Box

All pots are nested inside thick double-wall heavy corrugated shipping boxes engineered to withstand drops up to 1.5 meters.

2. Honeycomb Paper Wrap

No plastic bubble wrap. We cushion vessels in expandable hex-cut honeycomb brown paper which absorbs structural shock perfectly.

3. Zero-Risk Breakage Pledge

In the rare event that a ceramic piece suffers a fracture during transit, email a photo to our concierge and we will ship a free replacement instantly.
