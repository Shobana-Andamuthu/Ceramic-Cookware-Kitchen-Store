HERITAGE

A Tradition Born in Sanganer Pottery Studios

Founded in 2016, Ceramic Kitchen began with a simple belief: cookware shouldn't be mass-stamped from metals coated in synthetic Teflon. Every clay bowl and Dutch pot we throw is wedged by hand and vitrified in wood-fired kilns for 18 hours.

EARTHENWARE CARE

The 4-Step Clay Seasoning Ritual

Prepare raw terracotta & glazed earthenware for decades of non-toxic slow cooking.

01

Submerge in Pure Water

Soak unglazed clay fully in cold water for 12 hours allowing pores to hydrate completely.

Duration: 12 Hours
02

Rub Cold-Pressed Oil

Coat inside and exterior walls with unrefined sesame or mustard oil to seal micro-capillaries.

Organic Botanical Oil
03

Simmer Rice Starch Kanji

Simmer starch-rich rice water over low heat for 20 mins to form a natural non-stick glaze barrier.

Natural Non-Stick Barrier
04

Natural Sun Cure

Allow the pot to air-dry completely under warm indirect sunlight before your first feast.

Ready For First Feast
